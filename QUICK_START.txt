🚀 QUICK START - Deploy in 10 Minutes!
========================================

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
----------------
✓ You have FTP/cPanel access to broken.semprog.de
✓ You have your App Store and Google Play URLs ready
✓ You can create/edit images for favicon and social preview


STEP 1: DOWNLOAD FILES (1 min)
-------------------------------
Download all files from the /landing folder


STEP 2: CREATE IMAGES (5 min)
------------------------------
Create 3 images:

favicon.png (32x32 or 64x64)
→ Simple square icon of your logo
→ Use Canva, Photoshop, or online favicon generator

app-icon-180.png (180x180)  
→ Clean logo for iOS home screen
→ Export from your app's actual icon

og-image.png (1200x630)
→ Social media preview image
→ Include logo + tagline "Heal Together"
→ Use dark background with purple/blue gradient


STEP 3: UPDATE APP LINKS (2 min)
---------------------------------
Open index.html and find/replace:

Line 24:  YOUR_APP_ID
→ Replace with your actual Apple App Store ID

Line 208: https://apps.apple.com/app/broken
→ Replace with your actual App Store URL

Line 219: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.broken.app
→ Replace with your actual Google Play URL


STEP 4: UPLOAD TO SERVER (2 min)
---------------------------------
Using FTP or cPanel:
1. Connect to broken.semprog.de
2. Go to /public_html/ folder
3. Upload ALL files:
   - *.html files
   - styles.css
   - script.js
   - robots.txt
   - sitemap.xml
   - .htaccess
   - favicon.png
   - app-icon-180.png
   - og-image.png


STEP 5: SET UP SSL (Auto or 1 min)
-----------------------------------
Most hosts auto-enable SSL.
If not:
1. Go to cPanel → SSL/TLS
2. Enable Let's Encrypt for broken.semprog.de
3. Wait 2-5 minutes


STEP 6: TEST WEBSITE (2 min)
-----------------------------
Visit: https://broken.semprog.de

Check:
✓ Homepage loads
✓ Click "Privacy" - works
✓ Click "Terms" - works  
✓ Click "Support" - works
✓ View on phone - responsive
✓ Click email links - opens mail


STEP 7: SET UP EMAILS (2 min)
------------------------------
In cPanel → Email Accounts:
1. Create: support@broken.semprog.de
2. Create: privacy@broken.semprog.de
3. Forward both to your main email
4. Set up auto-responder (optional)


DONE! 🎉
--------
Your website is live at:
https://broken.semprog.de

Next steps:
□ Submit to Apple App Store (use https://broken.semprog.de/support)
□ Submit to Google Play (use https://broken.semprog.de/privacy)
□ Share on social media
□ Add to your app's About section


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Website doesn't load?
→ Wait 5 minutes, clear cache, try again
→ Check files are in /public_html/ not a subfolder

CSS looks broken?
→ Hard refresh: Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
→ Check styles.css uploaded correctly

HTTPS not working?
→ Enable SSL in cPanel
→ Wait 24 hours for DNS propagation
→ Contact hosting support

Email not working?
→ Check spelling of email addresses
→ Verify accounts created in cPanel
→ Test with different email client
