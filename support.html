Support Center

We're here to help. Find answers to common questions, crisis resources, and ways to contact our support team.

🚨 In Crisis? Get Immediate Help

If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, please reach out for help now:

  • Emergency Services: Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): Call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line (US): Text HOME to 741741
  • International Association for Suicide Prevention: Find your country's hotline
  • EU Standard Emergency: 112
  • UK Samaritans: 116 123
  • Germany Telefonseelsorge: 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222

Remember: Broken is a peer support app, not a crisis service. Always contact professional help in emergencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download the Broken app from the App Store or Google Play, open it, and follow the sign-up process. You'll need an email address to create an account.

Is Broken really free?

Yes! Broken is free to download and use. We offer premium subscription tiers with additional features, but core functionality is always free.

How do I post anonymously?

When creating a post, toggle the "Post Anonymously" option. Your username and profile information will be hidden from other users.

What are points and XP?

Points and XP (experience points) are earned by completing daily challenges, engaging with the community, and tracking your mood. They help you level up and unlock achievements. They have no monetary value and are for motivation only.

How do subscriptions work?

Premium subscriptions are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. They automatically renew unless canceled. You can manage your subscription in your app store account settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Broken → Cancel Subscription

Android: Google Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Broken → Cancel Subscription

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies. Contact Apple Support or Google Play Support for refund requests. We cannot process refunds directly.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Or email privacy@broken.semprog.de with your request. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Is my data private?

Yes. We take privacy seriously and use industry-standard security measures. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your data.

Can I report inappropriate content or users?

Yes! Use the report button on any post or profile. Our moderation team reviews all reports. You can also block users directly in the app.

What languages does Broken support?

Broken supports multiple languages including English, German, Spanish, French, and more. You can change your language in the app settings.

Is Broken a replacement for therapy?

No. Broken is a peer support platform, not a mental health service. We are not a substitute for professional therapy, counseling, or medical treatment. If you need professional help, please contact a licensed mental health provider.

The app is crashing or not working. What should I do?

Try these steps:

  • Force close and restart the app
  • Check for app updates in your app store
  • Restart your device
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • If problems persist, email support@broken.semprog.de with details about the issue

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a password reset link.

Can I change my username?

Yes! Go to Profile → Settings → Edit Profile to change your username.

How do Support Circles work?

Support Circles are private groups for deeper connection. You need to reach level 10 or have a premium subscription to create a circle. You can join circles at any time if invited or if they're public.

What's the daily XP limit?

Free users have a daily XP limit to encourage balanced usage. Premium subscribers have unlimited daily XP.
